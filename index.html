Munshi: Invoice/Bill Maker is built for small businesses in India. Create invoices, bills of supply, quotations and receipts on your phone, share a PDF on WhatsApp, and show a UPI QR code to get paid.

What it does

Documents in seconds

Invoices, bills of supply, quotations, receipts, delivery challans, purchase orders and credit notes, with GST worked out for you.

Your business, your look

Add your logo, signature and business details so every PDF looks like it came from your own shop.

Get paid faster

Show a UPI QR code, record payments as they arrive, and see what is still to collect.

Share anywhere

Send a PDF on WhatsApp or print it. Hindi, Marathi and English are supported.

Works offline

Your data stays on your phone. There is no account to create and nothing to log in to.

Safe backup

Optional daily backup to your own Google Drive. The last 7 days are kept, and only you can open them.
